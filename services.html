Products & Services

From one-off diagnosis to ongoing monitoring.

3 min read • Updated April 2026

01 / Monitoring

Persipica Pulse

Pulse runs automated cross-model audits across a single strategic brand or a full portfolio of managed workspaces. It tracks score movement, competitor movement, and algorithmic drift while keeping every run in an evidence ledger.

Flexible workspaces

Per-workspace configuration of company, category, website, location, competitors, target keywords, and Slack webhook. Suitable for a single strategic brand or a fully isolated multi-client setup.

Cross-model async audit jobs

Asynchronous audit jobs run against GPT-5.4 Mini, Sonnet 4.6, and Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite Preview across selected buyer journey stages.

Deterministic scoring pipeline

Versioned prompt catalog, provider adapters, shared web grounding for citation prompts, and centralised scoring.

Evidence Ledger

Every audit run logs the exact prompt used and the full AI output. Teams can review model behaviour and trace citation changes.

02 / Diagnosis

AI Presence Audit

The audit is a fixed-scope engagement that measures your brand's AI citation rate across the full buyer journey, maps competitor displacement, and delivers a prioritised action plan your team can execute immediately.

Stage-level citation coverage

Discovery, Comparison, Buying Intent, and Brand stage citation rates measured across GPT-5.4 Mini, Sonnet 4.6, and Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite Preview.

Technical gap analysis

Which competitors AI prefers, which prompts trigger them, and how your site scores on technical AI-readiness.

03 / Implementation

Full GEO Programme

Implementation support that turns audit findings into execution. This covers content restructuring, authority building, structural fixes, and a post-implementation re-audit to confirm progress.

  • Strategy & Roadmap A prioritised roadmap is built from the audit data. It covers content gaps, authority signals, and structural changes. Every recommendation ties back to a citation gap.
  • Execution Content restructuring, authority signal building, and entity definition work. All output is documented so your team can maintain it independently.
  • Re-Audit Confirmation Full GEO Programme clients receive a post-implementation re-audit to confirm progress and measure citation growth.
